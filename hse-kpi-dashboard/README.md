# HSE and Compliance KPI Dashboard (Power BI)

A Power BI dashboard that tracks health, safety and compliance performance for a fictional marine operator: incidents, audit and inspection findings, and corrective-action close-out.

> **All data in this project is synthetic.** It was generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` and does not describe any real company, vessel or person.

## What it answers
- How many incidents are we having, and is the trend improving? (including LTIFR and near-miss ratio)
- What are the main categories and root causes?
- What are audits and inspections finding, and how serious are the findings?
- Are corrective actions being closed on time, and where is the overdue backlog?

## Workflow
1. `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` creates raw data with realistic problems (duplicates, missing values, invalid dates).
2. `scripts/data_quality_checks.py` validates the raw data, logs every issue in `data/clean/dq_report.csv`, and writes cleaned tables.
3. The cleaned tables are loaded into Power BI, modelled with a date table, and reported through the measures in `docs/DAX_measures.md`.

## Data model
Tables: `locations`, `incidents`, `audits`, `findings`, `corrective_actions`, `exposure_hours`, `Dim_Date`, `dq_report`. See `docs/DAX_measures.md` for relationships.

## Key KPIs
LTIFR and recordable case frequency (per 1,000,000 hours), near-miss ratio, audit findings and high-risk share, open and overdue actions, on-time closure %, average days to close.

## Data-quality checks
Duplicate IDs, missing root causes, unknown locations, closed-before-raised dates, closed actions with no date, orphan findings. Results are shown on the dashboard's Data Quality page.

## Dashboard pages
1. Executive Summary
2. Incidents and Safety Performance
3. Audits and Findings
4. Corrective Actions
5. Data Quality
